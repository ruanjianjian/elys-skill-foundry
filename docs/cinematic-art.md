# Cinematic illustration assets

Created 2026-10-17 with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. No CLI or API-key workflow was used. Each image is an independent generation call. All deliverables were inspected visually. Production JPEG copies are published in `media/cinematic-art/`; full-resolution PNG masters are kept in the local task artifacts. `wide.png` was viewed locally before its use as the reference for subsequent shots.

All assets are original illustration-story keyframes for the ns-spec-cards film. No external reference artwork was supplied or copied. Original built-in output files remain in the default generated-images directory; the selected PNG masters remain in local task artifacts.

## wide.png — master wide shot

Reference inputs: none.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one original cinematic animation storyboard background for a 16-second promotional film about decision cards. Create a single full-bleed 16:9 landscape frame, no text anywhere, no collage or multi-panel layout.
Scene/backdrop: an independent designer's cluttered physical workbench, a bulky old monitor on the far left, piles of completely blank index cards and loose paper covering the near desk. Camera is a wide slightly high 3/4 view with a strong diagonal desk edge and credible perspective depth.
Subject: a young adult androgynous designer with short black hair, expressive angular tired face, wearing a vermilion/orange-red utility jacket and dark round over-ear headphones. The designer sits right of center, shoulders slightly hunched, one hand bracing their head and the other on the desk, visibly overwhelmed by paper. Keep this character original, with no resemblance to any existing animation character.
Composition/framing: character occupies the right third, desk and old monitor establish the workspace. Preserve the left-center middle area as quiet teal/navy negative space where real moving cards can later be composited; do not fill that region with tiny objects. Viewer's eye travels along desk perspective toward the character. Hands, hair silhouette and cast shadows should feel deliberately drawn.
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-drawn animation-film ink lines and screenprinted paper grain. Architectural planes, decisive ink marks, imperfect ink fills, restrained halftone, subtle print-registration offset. Strong authored graphic composition and tangible analog warmth, not generic stock illustration.
Lighting/mood: late-night focused work, broad theatrical shadow masses, one warm paper-colored lamp light across the work surface; frustration and compression rather than horror.
Color palette: only aged teal, deep inky navy, warm paper white and vermilion; flat rich limited-color print shapes, shadows from those colors. No rainbow accents.
Constraints: all paper/card/monitor faces blank, no typography, no words, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no rounded corporate mascot, no floating UI, no infographic, no glossy 3D, no glossy AI skin, no cyberpunk neon, no generic tech rings. Maintain clean readable silhouette while retaining hand-made screenprint texture.
```

## Visual acceptance

- Master: right-positioned designer, blank CRT, blank paper cards, large clear left-center wall region, limited teal/navy/paper/vermilion palette.
- Eye: identity, short black hair, dark circular headphone and vermilion collar remain recognizable; one eye dominates the composition.
- Hand: complete warm-paper portrait card with blank face and all four corners visible; fingers grip the edge and leave a large area for compositing.
- Clean: same room/camera and wardrobe, upright calm posture, hands lowered to table, central clutter removed and exactly three blank cards left in a row.

The assets are near-16:9 landscape. For the held card at the 1672 × 941 source resolution, approximate corner positions are TL(551,122), TR(942,107), BR(965,656), BL(574,674); keep UI inset from edges and away from the thumb grip at lower right.
